# Gerson Lehrman Group (GLG)

> GLG (Gerson Lehrman Group) is the world's leading platform for trusted human expertise, connecting organizations with a network of senior-level subject-matter experts to inform strategic decision-making. For more than 25 years GLG has facilitated expert calls and meetings, on-demand expert content and transcripts, research and surveys, events, and advisory and placement engagements. Its myGLG platform layers AI-powered and agentic research tools over that expert network.
